# WordPress Agency Website Build

> **Case study** · Web design & development · Client: digital agency (name kept private)

## Overview
A digital agency needed a brand-new website. The brief was to follow the structure of a
leading competitor, write fully original content, and use only free tools.

## Research
- Reviewed a shortlist of competitor websites and picked the strongest one as the structural model
- Mapped the competitor's home page sections and menu so the new site covers the same ground

## What I built
- **4 pages:** Home, About Us, Services and Contact Us
- **Home page:** hero with a quote form, client logo strip, intro, featured projects, testimonial
  and call-to-action bands, industries, services, a 6-step process, "why us", FAQ and a closing CTA
- **About Us:** story, mission/vision/values, what we do, stats, how we work, team and contact
- **Services:** one page with a section for each of 7 services, each with its own anchor link
  so the menu can jump straight to it, plus process, FAQ and contact
- **Contact Us:** contact cards, a full enquiry form, "what happens next" and FAQ
- **Mega menu:** a full-width Services panel with three columns and a promo card,
  becoming a slide-out menu on mobile
- **Forms:** two Contact Form 7 forms, a short Quick Quote form and a full Project Inquiry form
- **Global header and footer** applied site-wide

## Branding
- Extracted the colour palette directly from the client's logo
- Applied it to every page, the mega menu, forms, buttons and footer
- Adjusted a light button gradient so white button text stays readable

## SEO setup
- Rank Math configured for a professional service business, with the XML sitemap on
- A unique SEO title, meta description and focus keywords for every page
- Clean permalinks (e.g. `/about-us/`), site title, tagline, time zone and discussion settings

## Tools
WordPress · Astra · Elementor (free) · ElementsKit Lite · Contact Form 7 · Rank Math

## Notes
No testimonials, statistics or case studies were invented. Placeholder blocks were left
for the client's real data.
